# 🔍 How to Find Your cPanel URL

## Method 1: Check Your Email 📧 (EASIEST)

After buying hosting, SiteGround sends an email with the subject:
- "Welcome to SiteGround!" or
- "Your cPanel Login Details" or
- "Account Information"

**Look for:**
- **cPanel URL:** Usually looks like:
  - `https://123.456.789.1:2083` (IP with port 2083)
  - OR `https://cpanel.yourdomain.com`
  - OR `https://yourdomain.com:2083`
- **Username:** Usually your email or something like `mypaypa1`
- **Password:** The one you created during signup

---

## Method 2: Login to SiteGround Client Area 🌐

1. Go to: https://client.siteground.com/
2. Login with your SiteGround email & password
3. Click on **"Websites"** tab
4. Find your domain: **mypaypalwallet.com**
5. Click **"Site Tools"** button
6. This opens your control panel (similar to cPanel)

**Alternative in Client Area:**
- Look for **"cPanel"** button or **"Go to cPanel"** link
- Click it to access your cPanel directly

---

## Method 3: Common cPanel URLs for SiteGround

Try these URLs (replace with your domain):

```
https://mypaypalwallet.com/cpanel
https://mypaypalwallet.com:2083
https://cpanel.mypaypalwallet.com
```

Or use SiteGround's Site Tools (newer interface):
```
https://my.siteground.com
```

---

## Method 4: Contact SiteGround Support 💬

If you can't find it:
1. Go to: https://www.siteground.com/
2. Click **"Chat"** button (bottom right)
3. Say: "I need my cPanel URL for mypaypalwallet.com"
4. They respond in 1-2 minutes

---

## 📝 What to Do After Finding cPanel URL:

1. **Open the cPanel URL** in your browser
2. **Login** with your username & password
3. **Look for "File Manager"** (usually under "Files" section)
4. **Reply here with:** "I'm in cPanel" or "I see File Manager"

Then I'll guide you through uploading your files! 🚀

---

## ⚡ Quick Tip:

SiteGround now uses **"Site Tools"** instead of traditional cPanel for new accounts.

**Site Tools has:**
- File Manager (same functionality)
- MySQL (for databases)
- PHP Settings
- SSL Certificates

It's actually easier than cPanel! Just go to:
**https://client.siteground.com → Websites → Site Tools**
