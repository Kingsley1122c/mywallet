========================================
SETUP NGROK - TRUSTED PUBLIC ACCESS
========================================

STEP 1: Create Free Ngrok Account
----------------------------------
1. Go to: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/signup
2. Sign up with your email (100% FREE)
3. Verify your email

STEP 2: Get Your Auth Token
----------------------------
1. Go to: https://dashboard.ngrok.com/get-started/your-authtoken
2. Copy your authtoken (looks like: 2abc123def456...)

STEP 3: Configure Ngrok
------------------------
Open PowerShell and run:

cd C:\Users\HP\Desktop\ngrok
.\ngrok.exe config add-authtoken YOUR_TOKEN_HERE

(Replace YOUR_TOKEN_HERE with your actual token)

STEP 4: Start Your Site
------------------------
1. Make sure PHP server is running (it should be)
2. Run this command:

cd C:\Users\HP\Desktop\ngrok
.\ngrok.exe http 8000

STEP 5: Share Your Link
------------------------
You'll see a URL like: https://abc123.ngrok-free.app
This URL:
- ✅ Won't show security warnings
- ✅ Works on all devices
- ✅ No verification pages
- ✅ Stays active while ngrok is running

========================================
Note: Free ngrok gives you a random URL each time.
If you want a permanent URL, upgrade to paid ($8/month).
