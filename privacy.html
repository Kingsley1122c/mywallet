Privacy Policy

At Mivonta, we take privacy seriously. This notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to registered customers who use this account-access service.

Your Trust Matters: We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information stored and processed by this service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you register for and use Mivonta, we collect:

Data Type Examples Purpose
Personal Information Name, email address, phone number Account creation, identity verification
Financial Information Bank account details, card information Processing transactions
Transaction Data Send/receive amounts, dates, recipients Service delivery, history tracking
Communication Data WhatsApp messages, support emails Customer support, service improvement

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent
  • Session Data: Login times, authentication tokens (encrypted)
  • Location Data: Country/region selection, IP-based location

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide Services: Process transactions, manage your account, provide customer support
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Detect suspicious activities, verify identity, prevent unauthorized access
  • Communications: Send transaction confirmations, security alerts, service updates via email
  • Compliance: Meet applicable legal obligations and respond to valid requests
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Currency Conversion: Display balances in your preferred currency using live exchange rates

3. Information Sharing

We Never Sell Your Data: Mivonta does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Infrastructure, hosting, email delivery, exchange-rate, and security providers that help us operate the service
  • Connected Financial Partners: Parties involved in processing an account action when that feature is enabled for your account
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or to prevent fraud/crime
  • Business Transfers: In the event of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Data Security

We implement practical security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Sensitive stored data such as passwords is protected using current application controls
  • Secure Connections: Public deployments should be configured to use HTTPS for account traffic
  • Password Hashing: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (we cannot see your password)
  • CSRF Protection: Tokens prevent cross-site request forgery attacks
  • Session Management: Secure session handling with automatic timeout
  • Admin Approval: All transfers require admin verification before completion
  • Audit Logging: All sensitive actions are logged for security review

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide services
  • Required by law, contract, or a legitimate business need tied to the service
  • Necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements

After account closure, we may retain anonymized data for analytical purposes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

Right Description How to Exercise
Access Request a copy of your personal data Contact customer service
Correction Update incorrect or incomplete information Edit in account settings or contact us
Deletion Request deletion of your account and data Email support or use the contact option shown in your account
Opt-Out Unsubscribe from marketing emails Click unsubscribe link in emails
Data Portability Receive your data in a portable format Request from customer service

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Session Management: Keep you logged in securely
  • Preferences: Remember your language and currency selection (stored in localStorage)
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraudulent activity

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

Exchange Rate API

We use third-party APIs to fetch live currency exchange rates. These services may collect anonymized usage data but do not receive your personal information.

Support Channels

If you choose to contact support through a third-party messaging tool or email provider, that provider may process the communication under its own privacy terms.

Unsplash

Images on our website are sourced from Unsplash. Unsplash's privacy policy applies to image delivery.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers in different countries. We ensure adequate safeguards are in place to protect your data in compliance with applicable laws.

10. Children's Privacy

Age Restriction: Mivonta is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

If we discover that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately. Parents who believe their child has provided information should contact us.

11. Email Notifications

We send email notifications for:

  • Transactional Emails: Add money confirmations, transfer notifications, withdrawal updates (cannot opt out)
  • Security Alerts: Suspicious activity, password changes, login from new device (cannot opt out)
  • Service Updates: Changes to Terms or Privacy Policy (cannot opt out)
  • Marketing Emails: Optional promotional content (can opt out)

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via:

  • Email notification to your registered address
  • In-app notification upon login
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page

Your continued use of Mivonta after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data:

  • Email: support@mivonta.com
  • Support Window: We aim to respond within two business days

14. Policy Updates

We review this notice as the service changes and update it when our data handling practices materially change.
